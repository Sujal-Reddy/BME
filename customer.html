How this comparison works
  • A week is seven days and a comparison month is thirty days. Rental rates are prorated; actual minimum hire periods may differ.
  • Loan payments and ownership upkeep are counted in whole months, rounding partial months up.
  • Rental cash outflow includes the deposit before any refund. Add transport, fuel, operator, tax and other agreed costs to the relevant extra-cost field.
  • Purchase figures cover project-period cash outflow only. They exclude remaining loan balances, resale value, depreciation and tax benefits, so they are not a total cost of ownership comparison.
  • Machine availability, operator inclusion, deposit, cancellation and replacement terms must be confirmed in your quote.
